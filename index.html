How to play · PC keyboard & all-screen controls

Move: WASD or arrow keys. Attack: Space. Quickstep: hold Shift (uses stamina). Guard/Parry: hold R; time it just before a melee strike. Interact: E near a shrine or stairs. Side-step: Q or C. Drink potion: H. Inventory: I. Map: M. Pause: P or Esc.

Goal: explore, defeat enemies, collect glowing loot, use shrines, and find the stairs. Every fourth floor has a guardian; defeat it to descend. Level up to choose talents. Death ends the current run.

Touchscreen: press and hold a D-pad direction to move; you can hold one direction while tapping an action. Tap Attack, Quickstep, Interact, or Potion. Controls have large touch targets and are usable on phones and tablets in portrait or landscape. If the page scrolls, use the game controls rather than swiping the page.

PC: use the keyboard or click the same on-screen controls with your mouse.
